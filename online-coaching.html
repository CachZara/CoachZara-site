Training that travels with you

Not near Chicago, or just prefer training on your own schedule? Online coaching gives you the same individualized programming as in-person training — built around your goals, equipment, and availability — with video form checks and regular check-ins to keep you accountable.

Whether your goal is strength & conditioning, toning, fat loss, or learning Olympic weightlifting technique remotely, plans are periodized and adjusted based on your progress, not a generic template.

How an online session works

We schedule your session ahead of time and train together live over FaceTime, WhatsApp, Zoom, or whichever video app you prefer. All you need is access to free weight equipment. Every session follows the same structure as in-person training:

  • 1. Warm-up — raise your heart rate and get your body ready to train.
  • 2. Mobility — targeted mobility exercises to open up the joints and positions you'll use that day.
  • 3. Strength — the main lifts of the session, with live coaching on technique and load.
  • 4. Conditioning — a workout to build your engine and endurance.
  • 5. Cool-down — bring your heart rate down and finish with recovery work.

Who online coaching is best for

  • Busy schedules — no commute to the gym, so it's easier to fit training into a tight week.
  • Living in another city — get the same coaching from anywhere in the USA.
  • People who prefer training online — train in your own space, with a coach right there on the call.
