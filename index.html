FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much does personal training cost in Chicago?

Personal training in Chicago usually costs between $85 and $150 per session. My in-person sessions range from $100 to $125 per session depending on the package, with a free bonus session (a $100 value) on the 15-session package and 2 free bonus sessions (a $200 value) on the 20-session package, semi-private sessions range from $120 to $150, Olympic weightlifting sessions range from $100 to $125 per session depending on the package with a free bonus session (a $100 value) on the 15-session package, and online coaching ranges from $76.67 to $85 per session with a free bonus session (a $80 value) on the 10-session package and 2 free bonus sessions (a $153.33 value) on the 15-session package. Your first session is a free trial.

Do I need experience to learn Olympic weightlifting?

No. We can start from scratch. I teach the snatch and clean & jerk step by step, starting with the basic positions and building up at your pace, so beginners are welcome.

How does online coaching work?

We schedule your sessions and train together live over FaceTime, WhatsApp, Zoom, or whichever video app you prefer. All you need is access to free weight equipment.

Where can I train with you?

In person in Chicago, either at your gym or mine. Online coaching is also available anywhere in the USA.
