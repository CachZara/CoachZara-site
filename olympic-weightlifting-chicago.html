Learn the lifts the right way

Olympic weightlifting looks intimidating from the outside, but it doesn't have to be. Coach Zara breaks the snatch and clean & jerk down into manageable progressions, so you build real technique from the ground up instead of guessing your way through it. Sessions happen in person at your gym in Chicago.

This isn't just for competitive athletes — plenty of clients come in with zero barbell experience and leave lifting confidently, understanding their body better, and moving safely in CrossFit classes or general strength training. If you are training for competition, programming includes technique drills and meet prep as well.

What's included

  • Snatch and clean & jerk progressions, taught step by step
  • In-person technique analysis and video feedback
  • Programming for beginners through competitive lifters
  • Competition prep for those pursuing a meet
