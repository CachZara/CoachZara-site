MEET COACH ZARA

Personal trainer and USA Weightlifting certified coach, working with clients of every age and level — in person in Chicago, and online anywhere in the USA.

I've tried, from day one, to treat every client the way I'd want to be treated — with patience, honesty, and real attention to detail. Nothing about your program is generic; every session and every rep is coached with intention, whether your goal is strength, toning, fat loss, or learning Olympic weightlifting.

The results my clients get are what prove I'm worth trusting — but that's never just me. It's teamwork. I'll always do my part, session after session, but you have to show up, stay consistent, and follow the nutrition it takes to get the results you actually want.

"I do my part. You bring the consistency. Together, that's what gets results."
